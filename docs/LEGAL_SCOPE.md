# Legal research scope

The design motivation is identified disputed funds and reasoned human review. This repository does not claim every whole-account restriction is unlawful or that model explanations satisfy court evidence requirements.

A retrieved judgment text: Sunil P.P. v. National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal, Kerala High Court, 29 January 2025, 2025:KER:8150:
https://indiankanoon.org/doc/26486258/

This third-party reproduction includes directions limiting freezes to amounts mentioned in police requisitions in the cases before the court. Verify signed court copies, operative paragraphs, jurisdiction, subsequent treatment and statutory powers before relying on it. The software does not encode a universal legal rule.

Retain complaint and authority references, identified transfers, tracing method and uncertainty, independently verified allocation, current balance, reasons, reviewer identity/date and review process. Synthetic labels and Shapley values do not substitute for those records.
